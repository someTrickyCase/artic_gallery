# ARTIC Gallery

Art deserves a little room to breathe. ARTIC Gallery is a quiet place to wander through the Art Institute of Chicago collection, follow your curiosity, and linger with the works that catch your eye.

A shifting mosaic of art invites you to explore at your own pace. Save the pieces you love and return to them whenever you like.
